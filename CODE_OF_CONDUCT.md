# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation respectful and harassment-free for everyone.
Show empathy, give constructive feedback, focus disagreement on ideas and
evidence, and take responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discriminatory
language, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and publishing
private information are unacceptable.

This policy applies in repository spaces and official project representation.
Maintainers may remove violating contributions and warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants.

For GitHub-hosted conduct, use the content's **Report** action or
[GitHub's private abuse-reporting form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
Do not open a public issue containing a private conduct report.

Adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
